Multi-Robot Active Cooperative Perception

Results of simulated experiments with ground robots in a forest (Ignition Gazebo, Fortress release). Code: kalhansb/hmr_explo.

  • Simulated experiment · 24 September 2026 Perceptive exploitation for support Robots parked as lookouts on the paths into a mulching site warn the mulcher of an approaching pedestrian about 22 s earlier than its own lidar.
  • Simulated experiment · 23 September 2026 Camera experiment: lesions on target oaks Two robots with an NDVI camera visit three randomly drawn target oaks after exploring; lesions on the targets are resolved far more often than those on undrawn oaks.
