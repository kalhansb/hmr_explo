NDVI-camera experiment · flatforest · two Huskies · 2026-09-23

Does visiting the targets let the camera see their lesions?

Each rendered run explores a ±25 m ROI until the 2D map is 90 % known (the explore-done cue), then releases three target oaks drawn at random from a pool of nine. The robots then visit a three-vantage ring on each target at 3.8 m. The camera is a 640 × 480 segmentation camera on each robot (the MicaSense RedEdge-MX field of view at half resolution), and NDVI is looked up per label. 113 lesion plates of 4, 8 and 16 cm sit on 19 oaks. A lesion counts as resolved once one frame shows it at ≥ 25 px.

After the cue: newly resolved lesions, targets vs untargeted pool oaks

Conditional gain: of the lesions not yet resolved at the cue, the share resolved by the end. It cannot hit a ceiling, because a tree already covered at the cue drops out. The primary control is the six pool oaks that were not drawn, since they share the targets' eligibility geometry. p is where the drawn target set ranks among all 84 possible draws from the pool in that run. The pooled p is an exact randomisation test over the runs: every combination of the runs’ 84 possible draws is counted.

Coarse measures vs lesion resolution

M2 is the campaign scorer's observed fraction of the 0.2–1.2 m trunk cylinder on the 0.20 m voxel map, replayed from each run's bag. On most oaks it is already near 1 by the cue, so it has little room to move; where a target starts lower (Oak 4 in rep 2), exploiting it lifts lidar coverage too. The camera measure is still far from its ceiling on most trees at the same moment. Whole-trunk bark pixels (the most bark any single frame shows; median over trees) move far less than lesion resolution. They record the closest pass, and exploration already drives past many trunks at 1–2 m. Lesions need every side of the trunk seen from close range, which is what the three-vantage ring adds. Every cell reads cue → end; T is the drawn targets, pool the undrawn pool oaks.

Method notes and deviations

  1. Sensor. Ignition Fortress segmentation camera (semantic), 640 × 480, HFOV 0.824 rad, 2 Hz, clip 0.1–40 m, mounted at base_link + (0.30, 0, 0.55) m, looking forward. NDVI is a per-label lookup, not a spectral simulation. What is tested is whether the robot puts enough pixels on each patch.
  2. Lesions. Six visual-only plates per oak, 2 × 4, 2 × 8 and 2 × 16 cm, at z 0.65–1.25 m, one per 60° sector, seed 2026. Each plate sits 2 mm proud of the bark's outer radius.
  3. Deviation 1: front views only. Fortress renders a plate's back face through its own trunk. Detections with the camera more than 90° off the plate normal are dropped (from ground-truth poses). The "back views kept" toggle shows the unfiltered numbers.
  4. Deviation 2: lesion 90 dropped. Lesion 90 (Oak 57, never a target) never renders, while the virtual camera sees it. It is out of all scoring, leaving 113 lesions. Oak 36's bark label also never renders (it comes out as background), which leaves lesion counts unaffected.
  5. Virtual camera. A pinhole model on ground-truth odometry, with plate clipping, a facing test, and occlusion by other trunks (r 0.40 m), pine crowns and stones. Per run it is checked against the rendered counts at the same frame times (acceptance: median best-pixel ratio 0.8–1.25 and ≥ 90 % agreement on resolved lesions).
  6. Partial exploitation counts as assigned. In rep 4 the south vantage of Oak 40 was never selectable, and both robots timed out on it after 300 s with only the north-east and north-west vantages dwelled. Its two unresolved lesions (61 and 62) face south, the side only that vantage would have seen. In rep 5 the same happened to Oak 42: its east-north-east vantage was never selectable, and the one missed lesion (64) faces that way. In rep 6 Oak 58 also ended partial (two of three vantages), but all its lesions were resolved anyway. Every target miss sits behind a vantage that was not reached. Runs are analysed by the drawn targets, so these shortfalls stay in the result.
  7. Where the new resolutions came from. The 53 target lesions resolved after the cue were first resolved from a median 3.5 m, the vantage ring. The 29 pool-oak lesions resolved after the cue were caught in passing from a median 9.6 m (cam_mechanism.py, reps 1–6). Rep 6 has the smallest contrast. It had the earliest cue (256 s), and its long exploit drive passed every pool oak at mid range, picking up 11 of their 21 open lesions.
  8. Pre-registration. Metrics, controls, tests and horizons were fixed in PLAN.md before the runs. The Phase A statistic was fixed before any campaign cell was scored. Every run is shown here, none excluded.
